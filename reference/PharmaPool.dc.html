Upload your data

Two files: 12 months of purchases (required) and today's inventory (optional). Messy spreadsheets are fine — we fix column names and drug spellings, and you check our work.
Step 1 · Compare

Your prices vs. benchmark

Each drug is priced against NADAC, the weekly CMS average of what pharmacies pay wholesalers. See savings by category, days of supply and FDA shortage alerts.
Step 2 · Swap

Equivalent swaps

Switch to an FDA-rated equivalent product from another manufacturer — same ingredient, strength and form — so you can join a buying round. You approve every swap before it's used.
Step 3 · Join

Buying rounds

1Opt in with a recommended commitment. Non-binding.
2The round must reach its volume target. If it misses, nothing is ordered.
3You get 48 hours to confirm or drop out.
Review & submit

Award

Everything you did on Compare, Swap and Join is collected here, plus supplier awards. Nothing is sent until you submit.
